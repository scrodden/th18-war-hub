Where to spend first

TH18 upgrade priority

Ore and Dark Elixir are the bottleneck. Don't spread them thin — follow this order so you're war-ready fast.

  1. One functional loadout per hero — epics first. Get the ★ picks above to a usable level before touching anything else: Queen Action Figure, Warden Fireball, RC Electro Boots, King Spiky Ball. These epics swing attacks more than any single upgrade.
  2. Then the second piece of each loadout. Round out the meta combos (Magic Mirror, Meteor Staff, Rocket Backpack, etc.) so every hero has two working pieces.
  3. Hero levels (Dark Elixir). Higher heroes = stronger abilities and more HP to tank. Keep all heroes upgrading whenever a builder/DE is free — they're the backbone of every attack.
  4. Lab — only the troops you actually run. Prioritize the current meta: Throwers, Root Riders, Healers, Dragons/Dragon Riders, Rocket Balloons. Don't waste lab time on troops you never bring.
  5. Key defenses next. The new TH18 defenses first — Revenge Tower, the merged Super Wizard Tower, Multi-Gear Tower, then Inferno/Eagle/Monolith. (See the defenses page for why.)
  6. Everything else. Walls last, as always — they're cheap value only once the above is done.
Ore tip: Starry Ore (the rarest) only goes into epic equipment — spend it on the epics you use every attack (Fireball, Action Figure, Electro Boots, Rocket Backpack). Use the common Shiny/Glowy Ore on the standard pieces in your loadouts. Never dump Starry Ore into an epic you don't actually run.
