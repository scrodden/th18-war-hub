The fundamentals

The anatomy of every TH18 attack

Whatever army you bring, a three-star follows the same four beats. Learn the shape once and every strategy below just becomes a variation on it.

  1. Funnel first. Send cheap units or a hero down each side of your entry to clear the outer buildings. If you skip this, your army pathes around the base and never reaches the Town Hall — the #1 reason attacks fail at TH18.
  2. Charge a hero in. A Royal Champion (Electro Boots) or Queen (Giant Arrow + Healers) punches a lane toward the middle and softens the scariest defense before the main force commits.
  3. Commit the main force + spells. Drop your tanks and damage together so they arrive as one wave. Rage/Overgrowth/Invisibility go down the instant troops hit the first heavy defense — not before.
  4. Manage the tail. Save one spell and your hero abilities for the Revenge Tower and roaming Guardian, which get nastier as the base dies. Sloppy 95%-and-stall = a failed three-star.
TH18 twist: the Guardian patrols, so it can walk into your army mid-raid, and the Super Wizard Tower chains across clumped troops. Keep air units spread and time a Totem Spell or Invisibility to slip past the worst of it.
01

RC Walk Meteor Golem

Ground smashMedium

The most reliable ground three-star at TH18. The Royal Champion walks a full corner off the base to build a clean funnel, then Meteor Golems tank into the core while Super Yetis and support wreck compartments behind them.

  1. Pick your entry corner. Aim at the side with the fewest point defenses — you'll walk the RC along the edge from here.
  2. RC walk to funnel. Drop the Royal Champion (with Electro Boots) plus a couple of tanky units at the corner. Let her walk the outside, clearing buildings so the main army can't wrap. Feed her an Invisibility spell if a big defense targets her early.
  3. Send the Meteor Golems + Super Yetis. Once the funnel is open, commit your golems as the front wall. Their thrown Meteormites chip defenses over the first wall so you're doing damage before you even break in.
  4. Rage + Heal the push. Drop Rage when the pack hits the core and Healing when the Super Wizard Tower or Monolith starts melting them. Keep them moving as one blob.
  5. Save an Invis for the tail. Use it to slide past the roaming Guardian or the ramping Revenge Tower so the last 20% doesn't stall you out.
✓ Use it against
  • Centered Town Hall / deep ring bases
  • Heavy splash cores (bait them with golems)
  • Compartment-maze bases that punish air
✕ Avoid when
  • Town Hall is on the edge (over-kill for a snipe)
  • Bunched Giant Bombs + spring traps on the ground path

02

Zap Throwers (Dragon Duke)

HybridMedium

A surgical hybrid: Lightning + Earthquake spells blow open one dangerous defense cluster, a Dragon Duke with a Rocket Backpack darts in to finish softened defenses, and a wave of Throwers grinds through the hole you made.

  1. Pick your zap target. Find the one defense cluster that would shred your ground troops — usually a Multi-Gear Tower + Inferno or a Scattershot pair. That's what you're deleting.
  2. Zap it open. Stack your Lightning spells (plus an Earthquake) on that cluster before you deploy anything. You want it gone or one hit from gone.
  3. Funnel both shoulders. Use your King, Queen and a few troops to clear the corners either side of your entry so the Throwers march straight in.
  4. Rocket the Duke in. Send the Dragon Duke through the gap; the Rocket Backpack lets it leap onto the next priority defense and stay enraged. Keep it moving toward the core.
  5. March the Throwers + spells. Commit the Thrower line behind the Duke. Drop Rage on the cluster and Freeze the Inferno / Super Wizard Tower as they enter. The Log Launcher siege opens walls on the way.
✓ Use it against
  • Bases with one obvious "zap-able" defense knot
  • Layouts where the core is reachable once one cluster dies
✕ Avoid when
  • Defenses are spread out (nothing worth zapping)
  • Air defense is stacked around the Duke's lane

03

Electro Dragon Spam

AirBeginner-friendly

The easiest three-star to learn: Electro Dragons fire a chain-lightning bolt that arcs between buildings, so on a compact base they melt everything at once. Funnel both sides, then flood the middle.

  1. Funnel both flanks. Send the Dragon Duke down one side and the Queen (with Giant Arrow) down the other to shave the outside buildings. Air armies drift badly without this.
  2. Line up your Electro Dragons. Deploy them in a spread line along the funnel — never a tight stack — so the Super Wizard Tower can't chain through the whole pack.
  3. Add Balloons for the point defenses. A few Balloons peel off to kill Infernos / Air Defenses that would otherwise pick your dragons apart.
  4. Overgrowth & Rage the path. Drop Overgrowth to smother a defense (Monolith / Super Wizard Tower) and Rage to blitz the core. Freeze the Inferno the moment it locks on.
  5. Let the chain do the work. As buildings cluster in the core, each bolt hits more targets — don't over-manage, just keep the spells feeding the push.
✓ Use it against
  • Compact bases (buildings close = more chain targets)
  • Weak or poorly-spread Air Defense
  • When you want a low-micro attack
✕ Avoid when
  • Air Defense + Air Sweepers are stacked and centered
  • Very spread bases (chains fizzle)

04

Queen Charge Super Bowlers

HybridAdvanced

The high-skill, high-reward classic. A Queen Charge backed by five Healers carves through the base and takes the Town Hall plus key defenses; once she's recalled, Super Bowlers smash a core that's already been gutted.

  1. Plan the Queen's path. Pick a side where she can hit the Town Hall and one or two heavy defenses (Eagle, Inferno) without wandering. This is the whole attack — plan it before you deploy.
  2. Charge with 5 Healers. Drop the Queen, then her Healers a beat later so they lock on. Use Giant Arrow to snipe a defense and Rage/Heal to keep her alive through the core.
  3. Support the charge. A couple of tanky units and the King protect the Queen's flank and widen the lane she's cutting.
  4. Recall / finish the charge. When she's taken the TH and the key defenses, bring the main force online — the base is now missing its teeth.
  5. Super Bowlers smash the core. Send the Super Bowlers into the compromised middle with Rage down for maximum damage; the Warden ability shields them through the last few defenses and the ramping Revenge Tower.
✓ Use it against
  • Anti-air bases (this is pure ground)
  • Exposed Eagle / key defenses the Queen can reach
  • Somewhat open or edge Town Halls
✕ Avoid when
  • You're not confident reading the Queen's pathing
  • The core is a tight maze that stalls Bowlers

05

Hydra

AirAdvanced

A pure-air powerhouse: Ice Hounds tank at the front while Dragons and Dragon Riders follow behind, with Overgrowth controlling pathing and soaking damage. Two heroes funnel opposite flanks and the pack rolls the core.

  1. Funnel from both corners. The Dragon Duke takes one flank, the Queen the other. You need both shoulders clean so the air pack drives to the middle, not around it.
  2. Ice Hounds first. Drop the Ice Hounds as the front line. They tank the Air Defenses and, when they pop, drop a slowing pool that protects everything behind them.
  3. Dragons + Dragon Riders behind. Deploy them spread right behind the hounds so the Super Wizard Tower and Air Sweepers can't catch the whole group at once.
  4. Overgrowth to steer & shield. Drop Overgrowth on the defense you want smothered and to nudge the pack's pathing toward the core. Rage/Freeze for the final defenses.
  5. Warden up, finish clean. Pop the Warden ability entering the core and save a spell for the roaming Guardian and Revenge Tower so the tail doesn't cost you the third star.
✓ Use it against
  • Bases without stacked, centered Air Defense
  • Layouts you can funnel cleanly from two sides
✕ Avoid when
  • Air Defenses + Sweepers are centralized and high-level
  • You can't get a clean two-sided funnel
