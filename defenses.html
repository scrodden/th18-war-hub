Defense fundamentals

Why a good TH18 base holds — in plain English

Every layout in the vault is built from the same handful of ideas. Understand these and you can tell a great base from a pretty one, and tweak any layout to your clan's meta.

🎯

Centered Town Hall, deep rings

Burying the Town Hall behind several wall layers forces attackers to grind through the whole base to three-star. An edge Town Hall gets sniped instantly by a Royal Champion charge or a blimp — so it lives in the middle.

🧱

Compartmentalization

Chopping the base into many small walled boxes means every push needs a fresh wall-break and can only threaten one box at a time. It strangles Super Bowler and Yeti smashes and punishes any army that skipped its funnel.

💥

Splash defenses on the clump lanes

Monolith, Scattershots, the new Super Wizard Tower and Eagle Artillery sit where troops naturally bunch. Clustered Root Riders and Yetis get area-wiped; packed Electro Dragons and Hydra get chain-zapped. The base forces attackers to spread — which cuts their damage.

✈️

Spread & centered air defense

Air Defenses in a wide diamond plus centered Air Sweepers deny air armies a clean lane and blow packs off the core — so Electro/Hydra can't just float to the middle untouched.

🌀

Multi-Gear Tower in the core

It cycles between ground and air modes, so one central building threatens every archetype and never leaves a "free" army type.

TH18's new deterrents

The three defenses that punish a sloppy attack

🛡️ The roaming Guardian

Smasher or Longshot patrols a route, so it can physically walk into a stalled push and reset it. Attackers can't fully pre-plan around a moving heavy unit — and Smasher rages when the Town Hall dies, right when the attacker is trying to clean up.

📈 The Revenge Tower

It gains power and hitpoints as your base dies (at 5, 20 and 40 buildings destroyed). That turns every 90%-and-stall attack into a failed three-star — it directly punishes the most common attacker mistake: mismanaging the tail.

⚡ The Super Wizard Tower

Its bolt chains across up to 15 units. Any air army or swarm that clusters — Electro Dragon spam, Hydra, Balloons — feeds it. Good bases place it where those armies are funneled to bunch up.

How this beats the meta attacks: a centered ring + trapped funnels drags out the RC/Meteor-Golem ground smash while the Monolith melts the golem clump; spread air + Super Wizard Tower break up Electro/Hydra air packs; well-protected key defenses deny the Queen Charge cheap value; and spreading your strong defenses means no single Zap-Thrower cluster can delete the core in one shot. The Revenge Tower and Guardian clean up whatever slips through.
