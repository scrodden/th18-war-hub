The Crash Lands update

What Town Hall 18 changed — and why the meta shifted

TH18 literally crash-lands from a meteor, and it brought a batch of new defensive toys plus a new troop and spell. You can't attack or defend well without knowing these five things.

🛡️

Guardians (Smasher & Longshot)

TH18's signature defense. Only one Guardian patrols at a time. Smasher is a melee splash unit that rages when the Town Hall falls and drops rage on death; Longshot is a ranged splash unit that explodes when killed. They roam patrol routes, so they punish slow, clustered pushes.

📈

Revenge Tower

A TH18-exclusive defense that gets stronger as your base dies — it gains power and hitpoints as the 5th, 20th, and 40th buildings are destroyed. It turns sloppy 90%-and-stall attacks into failed three-stars late in the raid.

⚡

Super Wizard Tower (merged)

Two Wizard Towers merge into one tower whose bolt can chain across up to 15 units. Brutal against tightly-packed air and swarm armies — spreading your troops matters more than ever.

☄️

Meteor Golem (new troop)

A high-HP golem made of two "Meteormites" that it throws over walls to deal damage inside compartments, then re-merges. It's the new backbone of TH18 ground smashes because it tanks and reaches past the first wall layer.

🪧

Totem Spell (new spell)

A taunt/decoy: buildings and troops in its radius attack the totem and ignore everything else for a few seconds. Perfect for baiting a Monolith, Inferno, or Guardian off your real troops at the exact right moment.

🎖️

Heroes & equipment rule everything

At TH18 the single biggest factor isn't your army — it's hero levels and equipment (Electro Boots, Rocket Backpack, Giant Arrow, etc.). Underleveled heroes fail with the best army in the game. Get equipment functional first, then pick a strategy.

One rule beats them all: match the army archetype to the base's weakness instead of forcing your favorite. A ring base with a centered Town Hall wants a ground smash; a compact base wants Electro Dragons; an anti-air base wants Queen Charge ground. The strategy pages below tell you exactly when to use which.
One-tap imports

How the downloads drop straight into Clash of Clans

Every army and every base on this site is a real Supercell deep link. Open this page on the phone or tablet that has Clash of Clans installed, tap a button, and the game takes over.

⚔️ "Copy Army" links

Tapping an army button opens Clash of Clans on the army / quick-train screen and offers to copy the whole composition — troops, spells, siege machine, and even the hero equipment loadout.

  • Nothing is spent automatically. The game just fills the quick-train slot; you tap "Train" yourself.
  • If you don't own a troop level yet, the game just trains the highest you have — so links still work while you finish your lab.
  • On a desktop browser the tap won't open the game. Use the Copy link button and paste it into a chat you can open on your phone.

🛡️ "Open Layout" links

Tapping a base button opens the Layout Editor in Clash of Clans with the full design loaded. From there you can save it and set it as your War/CWL base.

  • Open the link → tap the layout → it loads into your editor → Save, then choose "Set as War Base" for Clan War League.
  • Walls, defenses and traps all come pre-placed; you only re-place anything the editor can't map 1:1.
  • These are real, publicly-shared layouts — see the honesty note at the bottom of the defenses page on where they come from and why base links can't be hand-built.
